What stays, and what goes?

    Copy as CSV copies the rows that match your filters, with the verdict, rule, reason and target for each. The redirect map lists every Merge and Retire URL with where it should point.

    Every page gets one verdict. The rules run in this order, and the first one that matches wins:

    1. Protected pages, and pages too new to judge, get Keep.
    2. Retire: low traffic, no conversions, old, and few backlinks.
    3. Merge: a near-duplicate title on the same topic with a stronger sibling, or a dead page with backlinks worth keeping.
    4. Refresh: still earning traffic or conversions, but declining, old, thin or converting weakly.
    5. Everything else gets Keep.
